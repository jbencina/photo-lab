# Filters and the dock

The dock is the floating bar at the bottom of the library. It is the same on phone and desktop.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shuffle** | Random order. Tap it again to reshuffle. |
| **By date** | Sort by date taken. Tap it again to flip between newest and oldest first. |
| **Liked** | Only the photos you liked. |
| **Color** | Search by color: preset swatches or a custom color. |
| **Subjects** | Filter by subject. |
| **More** | Exposure presets, dates, camera and lens, other sort orders, thumbnail size and exact ranges. |

Shuffle and By date are hidden for ranked results (query, color and similar search), which are always
best match first.

## Active filters

Active filters appear as removable chips above the grid, and the Color, Subjects and More buttons show
what is set on them. **Clear filters** removes them all.

![Three active filters: the Bird subject, the Tele preset and 2025](/img/screenshots/filters-active.jpg)

## Color

Sixteen preset swatches and a custom color picker. The library is ranked by how much of that color each
photo contains; see [Color search](library.md#color-search).

![The Color panel](/img/screenshots/dock-color.jpg)

## Subjects

Every subject in use, grouped by category, with its photo count. The tags you created are under
**Your tags**. Type in the find box to narrow the list; Enter picks the first match. Selecting several
subjects matches photos that have any of them.

## More filters

![The More filters panel](/img/screenshots/dock-more.jpg)

- **Presets**, each with its photo count:
  - Lens: wide (under 35 mm), normal (35–70 mm), tele (70–300 mm), super-tele (300 mm and up).
  - Light: bright (ISO 400 or lower), dim (ISO 500–2500), low light (ISO 3200 and up).
  - Depth of field: shallow (ƒ/2.8 or wider), medium (ƒ/3.2–ƒ/7.1), deep (ƒ/8 or narrower).
- **Date taken**: a histogram of years, then of the months of a year. Tap a month and then another for
  a range.
- **Camera** and **Lens**.
- **Sort by**: the other sort orders (date added, file name, date liked).
- **Thumbnails**: small or large.
- **Exact ranges**: type ISO, aperture, focal length and date ranges yourself.

Dates use the camera's local time as recorded in EXIF.
